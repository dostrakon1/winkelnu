# M0.43 — Feed Quality Thresholds & Operator Attention Signals

Status: repository-side implemented; exact HEAD CI pending.

## Delivered
- conservative read-only feed quality thresholds;
- minimum sample volume before ratio alerts;
- `healthy`, `watch`, `attention` and `critical` signal levels;
- exact merchant + feed correlation;
- attention events integrated into the operational feed timeline;
- unit tests for sample-size protection, reject criticality and pending-review backlog;
- no mutations, RPCs, schema changes or privilege expansion.

## Defaults
- >= 100 observed records required for ratio-based alerts;
- reject >= 5% attention, >= 15% critical;
- review load >= 10% attention, >= 20% critical;
- >= 25 pending reviews yields watch when no stronger signal applies.
